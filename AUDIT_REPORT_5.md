- The two lessons with a `#[cfg(test)]` module (9.1 and 9.2) also had that module compiled with `--test` and run; all 3 tests in each pass.

Also done: the validation notes in `RUST/VALIDATION.md` now say that the earlier CI result applied to the old lesson text and must be repeated, and the JSON records the rewrite and its limits. Generator scripts are in `rust-generator/`.

## Limits

- **The repository's own Rust checker was not run.** `scripts/check_rust_examples.py` uses edition 2024, and the only compiler available here is Rust 1.75, which cannot compile it. My checks used edition 2021 with the same `-D warnings` flag. The examples avoid known 2024 changes, but a fresh CI run on the Rust 1.99 baseline is required before relying on them.
- The JSON helper (Lessons 8.5, 10.4, 11.2) was tested with a round trip and six malformed inputs only. Lesson 10.4 also includes a short date algorithm for UTC timestamps, tested by its use, not against a reference.
- The standard library has no random numbers, so Lesson 9.3 uses a small xorshift generator that is not secure; the lesson says so.
- Not tested with screen readers or braille displays.
