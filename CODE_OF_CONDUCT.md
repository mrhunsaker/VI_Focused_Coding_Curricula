# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, neurotype, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We value contributions from learners, educators, developers, accessibility practitioners, and people with lived experience of disability. This project is especially concerned with making computer science education more accessible to blind and visually impaired learners.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, constructive, and considerate.
- Use welcoming language and assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experiences, and communication styles.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and outcomes rather than people.
- Consider accessibility in discussions, examples, and proposed changes.
- Acknowledge contributions and give appropriate credit.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate misgendering or use of slurs.
- Dismissing or mocking disability-related access needs.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional educational community.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces, including GitHub Discussions, Issues, Pull Requests, code reviews, and project-related public communication when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainer through GitHub's private contact mechanisms. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue or discussion. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact GitHub Support or use another appropriate private channel rather than reporting it to the person involved.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. The maintainer will respect privacy and safety to the extent practical. The project may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the circumstances.

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code, acting fairly and consistently, and prioritizing participant safety.
